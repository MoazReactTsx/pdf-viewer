Colors & dark mode

Text and vector colors are re-mapped while the page is drawn, so images keep their original colors. Scanned PDFs (a page that is one big image) can't be recolored.

Text size

A PDF has a fixed layout, so “Page zoom” scales the whole page. Use Reader to resize only the text.

Filters

Filters are for viewing only; they are not written into the saved PDF.
